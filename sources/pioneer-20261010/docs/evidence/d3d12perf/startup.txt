Start-up measurements, RTX 5060 unless said, 2026-10-09, single-agent, AC power.
many_cubes --headless-bench 10, POCKET_AA=msaa POCKET_GTAO=off POCKET_OCCLUSION=off; ms.

1. Where Renderer::new's time went (master, one thread; a temporary timer around every
   create_render_pipeline / create_compute_pipeline, not committed): see
   pipelines-serial-dx12-rtx5060.txt and pipelines-serial-vulkan-rtx5060.txt.
   Direct3D 12: 59 pipelines, 952 ms of Renderer::new's 1,027 ms. Vulkan: 59 pipelines, 40 ms of
   99 ms. The largest on Direct3D 12: forward 117.5, forward (masked) 100.4, splat preprocess
   41.8, ocean 35.7, cull 35.2, TAA resolve_copy 34.5 and resolve_history 32.6, GTAO fs_main 32.5,
   occlusion late 32.2; the double-sided forward variants 1.4 to 1.6 (their shaders are the
   back-face culled ones', which wgpu-hal keeps in its per-device cache keyed by the HLSL).

2. Concurrent creation, how many tasks run at once (par.rs slots; a temporary environment
   override of the limit). Renderer::new on Direct3D 12, min / median:
     3 rounds:  limit 6: 221.2 / 226.6   8: 172.1 / 188.7   12: 178.6 / 181.9   16: 186.0 / 192.4
                64 (no limit in practice): 193.8 / 204.1
     2 rounds:  limit 1: 959.0 / 964.4   2: 520.8 / 524.2   4: 291.0 / 296.1
     5 rounds:  limit 8: 191.0 / 193.0   12: 184.5 / 187.6   16: 190.2 / 191.4
   Vulkan, 5 rounds: limit 1: 114.9 / 117.1   8: 38.9 / 58.7   16: 41.9 / 60.8
   The forward fragment shader alone takes 104-117 ms with nothing else compiling, 116-128 ms
   with 4 tasks at a time and 165-175 ms with 12 to 16 (hyper-threads and all-core clocks), so the
   critical path stays that compilation. Chosen: the hardware threads (16 here), the simplest;
   8 to 16 are within 3%.
   Timeline of one concurrent start (limit 12): pipelines-concurrent-dx12-rtx5060.txt (end = ms
   since Renderer::new began; the forward pair ends last, at 171 and 176 ms).
