Per-phase CPU time of Renderer::render, RTX 5060, 2026-10-09, single-agent, AC power.
A temporary timer (not committed) stamped the phases of render() and printed their average over
200 frames; ms. Phases: sync = applying the frame; uniforms = the per-frame uniform writes before
the command encoder; cull+shadows = encoder until the opaque pass (skinning, cull, lights,
cascades); opaque = recording the opaque pass; gtao/taa/splat; post+ui = bloom, display
transform, overlays, UI; resolve = timestamp resolve; finish = CommandEncoder::finish (wgpu-core
records the passes into Direct3D 12 or Vulkan command lists); submit = Queue::submit.
POCKET_AA=msaa POCKET_GTAO=off POCKET_OCCLUSION=off unless said.
